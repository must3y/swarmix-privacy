Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Swarmix does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising SDKs.

Your game progress (level, coins, upgrades and skins) is saved only on your own device and never leaves it. Deleting the app removes this data.

If this policy changes (for example if ads are added in a future version), this page will be updated before the change is released.
